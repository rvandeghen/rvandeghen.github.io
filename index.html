About

I am a computer vision researcher who recently completed a PhD at the University of Liège under the supervision of Prof. Marc Van Droogenbroeck. My research focuses on semi-supervised and self-supervised learning, video representation learning, and differentiable 3D reconstruction and rendering.

My thesis, Learning With Unlabeled Data, studies learning from unlabeled data for object detection, image representation learning, and video understanding. In October 2026, I will take up a postdoctoral research position funded by the F.R.S.-FNRS, focused on improving video-text model pretraining.
